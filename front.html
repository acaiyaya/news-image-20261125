TRUMP HITS 14 NATIONS
WITH NEW TARIFFS

US proposes duties of up to 20% on China and 15% on the EU after an 8-month Section 301 probe into excess factory capacity — markets tumble, Nasdaq −3.9%

The Trump administration proposed new tariffs on 14 major trading partners — China, the EU, Japan, India, South Korea and nine others — accusing them of flooding world markets with cheap goods. China faces the highest rate, 20% on top of roughly 25% from Trump's first term; the EU faces 15%. The duties follow an eight-month investigation and come weeks after midterm losses; a public hearing is set for 25 January. Governments worldwide condemned the move and US stocks fell sharply.
